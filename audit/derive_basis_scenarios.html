02 · Scenario A — ETH, ~6× total exposure

3× carry loop, plus 3× options only when vol pays

The base (always on): deposit weETH → borrow USDC → buy more weETH → repeat to 3× long; short 3× ETH-PERP against it. Per unit of equity: 3× funding + 3× staking − 2× borrow.

The overlay (regime-switched): up to 3× notional of short ETH options (delta-hedged strangles via the perp book), traded through RFQ — only while implied vol trades above realized. History says the switch matters more than the selling:

Model: daily delta-hedged short-vol accrual, ½(IV² − realized²), IV from Deribit DVOL less a 1.5-vol-point all-in concession for RFQ pricing and fees. Never a losing year switched; two losing years always-on.

The overlay rule ON when DVOL ≥ trailing 30-day realized vol + 3 pts · OFF otherwise · checked daily, no look-ahead · today: DVOL 50.5 vs RV 68.9 → OFF — the rule is currently keeping the book out of a negative-edge vol market, which is exactly its job.

Five years of the switch. Shaded = overlay live. It was on through most of the 2022–23 carry regime, selectively on in 2024–25, and off in the high-realized shocks where short vol bleeds.

Note the mid-2025 funding drought: four consecutive negative months, −16.8% peak-to-trough. At 6.5×, funding regime is the P&L. It recovered because the premium came back — that is the bet.

Options-financed leverage: the synthetic combo

The long leg does not have to be financed from the USDC pool. A deep-ITM call bought together with a same-strike, same-tenor put sold is a synthetic forward: the put premium rebates the call's time value, the two legs' vega cancels — no exposure to any specific tenor's vol — and the all-in financing cost is the options-implied forward rate. Measured across Derive's live chain, that rate is tight and stable at every tenor:

Against ~12% average funding on the short perp, an options-financed turn carries ≈ +7–8 points — comparable to pool borrowing at today's ~4%, with two structural differences: it does not consume the $64M USDC pool (no self-inflicted rate impact, no utilisation cap), and it is executed as a single RFQ package. Its cost is fixed for the tenor at entry rather than floating with pool utilisation, and rolls at expiry re-price at the then-current forward basis.

05 · Constraints that size the trade

What bounds each scenario

Capacity · BTC

USDC pool depth caps pool-financed size near $1M equity at 6.5×

Derive's USDC market: $64M supplied, $42.5M borrowed (66% util). A 6.5× loop borrows 5.5× equity. Fitted rate curve from 1,200 real observations: ≤70% util ≈ 4%, 80–85% ≈ 7.2%, spikes to 17%. ~$1M equity keeps util under ~75%; $2M pushes borrow to ~7% and burns ~19 pts of carry. Options-financed turns (synthetic combos, above) sit outside this cap.

Margin · BTC

PM2 parameters decide whether 6.5× fits

With the in-account LBTC haircut published by the API (3.9%), a 6.5× loop fits with roughly half its IM in reserve. Under the documentation's conservative reading (~15% haircut) the ceiling is nearer 3.8×. The live account's own IM reading is the arbiter between the two.

Structural · both

Funding can invert; the venue has never seen a bear

Derive launched into a favorable regime — its entire history is one where shorts got paid. Worst observed 30-day funding: −1.0% (BTC) → −6.5% in a month at 6.5×. A 2022-style regime, which the venue has not yet experienced, would weigh materially heavier at full leverage.

Execution · both

RFQ is the real market for size

Resting books are thin (BTC-PERP ~$30k within 5% at snapshot). Options and size trade via RFQ — figures here assume fills ~1.5 vol pts inside DVOL, in line with private RFQ flow on the venue. Loop construction costs (perp entries plus sourcing weETH/LBTC where on-venue spot is thin) amortise to roughly 0.1–0.2% of notional, one-off.

Accounting note: all figures are carry-accounting (funding + staking + vol P&L − interest), the same convention throughout this engagement. Mark-to-market noise from perp basis and collateral pricing adds volatility around these paths but nets out over holding periods; it does not change totals.

06 · What drives the P&L

Four numbers carry both scenarios

Every result in this document reduces to four observable daily series — the strategies have no other inputs:

  • Realized funding on ETH-PERP and BTC-PERP — the dominant revenue line, ~12–13% APR on average over the venue's history, levered 3× to 6.5× across the scenarios.
  • USDC borrow APY and pool utilisation — the cost line for pool-financed turns, levered (L−1)×; the options-implied forward rate plays the same role for combo-financed turns.
  • DVOL − RV30 spread — the overlay's switch. Positive and wide: the overlay earns; negative (as at publication): the overlay stands aside.
  • Account IM utilisation — the binding constraint on total size; PM2's netting keeps it low precisely because the book is delta-neutral.
